# Accounts and login

## Password reset
Users reset a password from the login page using the "Forgot password" link. The reset email is valid for 60 minutes. If the email does not arrive, check the spam folder and confirm the address matches the account. Support cannot set a password on behalf of a user.

## Two-factor authentication
Two-factor authentication (2FA) is required for all account owners. Users can enrol an authenticator app or a hardware key. If a user loses their second factor, they must use one of their 10 recovery codes. Without recovery codes, identity verification takes up to 3 business days.

## Locked accounts
An account locks for 15 minutes after 5 failed login attempts. Repeated lockouts in one day trigger a security review, shown as error code AUTH-423. Support can lift a lock only after verifying the user's identity.

## Deleting an account
Account deletion is permanent after a 14 day cooling-off period. During that period the owner can restore the account from the confirmation email. Data exports must be requested before deletion.
