# Billing and refunds

## Refund policy
Customers on monthly plans can request a full refund within 14 days of a charge. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of purchase. After these windows, refunds are issued only as account credit. Refunds return to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.

## Double charges
A double charge usually means a payment retry succeeded after the first attempt was also captured. Support can confirm this in the billing console under Transactions. The duplicate is refunded automatically within 3 business days; if it is not, escalate to the payments team with error code PAY-409.

## Invoices
Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing cycle and emailed to the billing contact. To change the billing contact, an account owner must update it under Settings, then Billing, then Contacts. Past invoices can be downloaded as PDF for up to 7 years.

## Plan changes
Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing cycle. Moving from the Team plan to the Free plan deletes shared workspaces after a 30 day grace period.
